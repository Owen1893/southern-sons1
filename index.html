What we do

Landscape installation and irrigation are the core of what Southern Sons does, alongside the lighting and handyman work that rounds out a property.

Landscape Install

New beds, sod, plantings, mulch, and small hardscape — laid out and graded with drainage and mature growth in mind, not just how it looks on install day.

  • Planting beds, sod, & mulch
  • Tree, shrub & seasonal plantings
  • Edging & small hardscape
  • Wood decks & patio pavers

Irrigation

Sprinkler system installs, zone and valve repair, controller programming, and leak troubleshooting — so nothing goes thirsty, and nothing floods.

  • New sprinkler system installs
  • Zone, valve & head repair
  • Controller programming
  • Leak detection & seasonal check-ups

Lighting

Interior fixture installs and repair, plus exterior and landscape lighting — set up to highlight the yard you just had planted and watered.

  • Interior fixture install & repair
  • Porch & soffit lighting
  • Walkway & path lighting
  • Tree & architectural uplighting

Handyman Work

The in-between jobs that pile up on every homeowner's list — fence repair, gutter fixes, and small carpentry around the property.

  • Electrical repair & instal
  • Gutter cleaning & fixes
  • Small carpentry & mounting
  • General patch & repair jobs
